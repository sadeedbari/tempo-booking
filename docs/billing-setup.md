# Subscription billing: setup

Tempo charges businesses through Stripe Checkout and the Stripe Customer Portal (card details never touch Tempo).
Use **test mode** first. This is Tempo's own Stripe account, separate from the Connect flow that collects customer deposits.

## Pricing (CAD, monthly)
Base $15 (1 location, 5 staff). Extra location +$15 (+5 staff). Staff pack +$5 (+5 staff, shared). 14-day trial, no card.
Locations allowed = 1 + extra locations. Staff allowed = locations x 5 + packs x 5.

## Steps
0. **Choose `TRIAL_CLAIM_SECRET`** (see "Trials and teams"): a random string of 16+ characters, for example `openssl rand -base64 32`. Put it in your hosting provider's secret store and in `.env.local` for development. It is never stored in the database or in git. **Do not change it once in use.**
1. **Migrations.** `supabase/migrations/20261101000000_subscription_billing.sql` is **already applied to production** (it contains the read-only rules, the trial and team limits, and replaces the browser-callable `create_organization` with the server-only `create_organization_as`). **The step still to do is applying `supabase/migrations/20261102000000_billing_followups.sql`** (Supabase SQL editor or `supabase db push`; it sets a fixed `search_path` on `private.max_owned_orgs` and documents `forget_trial_claim`). **Then deploy the app.** The hosting provider must have `TRIAL_CLAIM_SECRET`, the three Stripe price ids (`STRIPE_PRICE_BASE`, `STRIPE_PRICE_EXTRA_LOCATION`, `STRIPE_PRICE_STAFF_PACK`) and `STRIPE_BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (steps 0, 2 and 3) before billing is switched on.
   Record the existing owners' trials, **once, before opening the app to new sign-ups**: `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL=... SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY=... TRIAL_CLAIM_SECRET=... node scripts/backfill-trial-claims.mjs` (hashes only; safe to run again). **This script is the only supported backfill path.** Do not paste the secret into the Supabase SQL editor (for example with `set_config`): the editor keeps it in its query history. Until the backfill has run, an existing owner could still earn one more trial.
2. **Create products and prices** (test key from your environment, nothing is written to disk):
   `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_test_... node scripts/setup-stripe-billing.mjs`
   Copy the three printed price ids into `STRIPE_PRICE_BASE`, `STRIPE_PRICE_EXTRA_LOCATION`, `STRIPE_PRICE_STAFF_PACK`.
3. **Webhook**: in Stripe, Developers > Webhooks > add endpoint `https://<your-app>/api/stripe/billing-webhook`, **listening to "Your account" events** (NOT "Connected and v2 accounts": the app refuses any event that carries an `account`, so a Connect-style endpoint would simply do nothing), with events
   `checkout.session.completed`, `customer.subscription.created`, `customer.subscription.updated`, `customer.subscription.deleted`,
   `invoice.payment_failed`, `invoice.paid`. Put its signing secret in `STRIPE_BILLING_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.
   (The existing `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` belongs to the Connect endpoint `/api/stripe/webhook`; do not reuse it.)
   For local testing: `stripe listen --forward-to localhost:3000/api/stripe/billing-webhook`.
4. **Customer Portal**: Stripe Dashboard > Settings > Billing > Customer portal: turn it on (allow updating payment methods and viewing invoices; leave plan changes and cancellation to Tempo's Billing page).
5. **Failed payments**: Settings > Billing > Subscriptions and emails: enable Smart Retries. Tempo gives 7 days of grace from the first failure, then pauses the business.
6. **Tax (optional)**: set up Stripe Tax, then set `STRIPE_BILLING_AUTOMATIC_TAX=1`.
7. Set `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, `CRON_SECRET` and `RESEND_API_KEY` (already used elsewhere): the webhook, the hourly job (trial reminders, expiries) and billing emails need them.
8. Redeploy. Owners find billing under Settings > Plan and billing.

## What happens
- Trial over / cancelled / unpaid past grace: dashboard read-only (view and export still work), public booking page and embed show "not accepting bookings", the database refuses new bookings, locations and team members. Nothing is deleted. Subscribing restores everything immediately.
- Cancel: access continues to the end of the paid period; can be resumed before then.
## Trials and teams
- **One free trial per person.** The first team a person creates, with a verified email address, gets the 14-day trial. A later team by the same person starts **without a trial**: it exists, can be viewed, and shows "Choose a plan"; subscribing switches it on. A person with an unverified email also starts without a trial (nothing is recorded, so verifying and creating another team still earns the trial). A paid subscription is never affected by these rules.
- **What is stored (S-012, PIPEDA).** `trial_claims` holds only `claim_hash` and `claimed_at`: no email, no user id, no team id. Per person there are two hashes, each an HMAC-SHA256 with `TRIAL_CLAIM_SECRET`: one of the normalised email (lower case, "+tag" ignored, and for Gmail also dots ignored) and one of the account id. Both must be new to earn a trial, so a second team under another spelling of the address is refused, and changing the verified email on the same account does not renew the trial (S-013). Each record is a keyed hash of an email address (and of an account id), which is **pseudonymous personal information, not anonymous data**: anyone who holds `TRIAL_CLAIM_SECRET` and the table can recompute the hash of a known email and see whether it has a record. Without the secret, the hashes cannot be reversed or tested against a guessed address.
- **Where the key lives.** Only in the `TRIAL_CLAIM_SECRET` environment variable of the server. Teams are created by the server (`create_organization_as`, callable only with the service-role key), which hands the key to the database for that one call; it is never saved in the database. If the secret is missing or shorter than 16 characters, **creating a team fails with an error** (fail closed): nothing is recorded or granted with an empty key.
- **Retention.** Records are **kept indefinitely, because that is their only purpose** (preventing repeat free trials). Deleting a team or an account does not remove them. Because the hashes are pseudonymous personal information, an erasure request is answered with `forget_trial_claim` (below), which removes that person's two records (they could then earn a new trial). **Flagged for your legal review** (and for the privacy policy, which is a separate item, S-011). If you ever change `TRIAL_CLAIM_SECRET`, every existing record stops matching and past trial users could claim again.
- **At most 3 teams per owner** (`private.max_owned_orgs()` in the migration and `MAX_OWNED_ORGS` in `src/lib/billing.ts`: change both). The 4th is refused with a clear message, both when creating a team and when someone is promoted to owner of one more (a database trigger). At the cap the onboarding page says so and links back to the dashboard, and the team menu does not offer "New team".
- **Production requirements (S-013).**
  1. Supabase **Authentication > Sign In / Providers > Email > "Confirm email" MUST be ON** in production (and staging). With it off, every email counts as verified and the verified-email check does nothing.
  2. The **`private` schema must not be exposed in the Data API** (Project Settings > API > "Exposed schemas": keep only `public`). The helper functions there are not callable by users, but they must not be reachable at all.
  3. Remaining limits, by nature of email-based rules: catch-all domains, disposable mailboxes and relay addresses (such as Apple "Hide My Email") can still get a trial each, and dots on non-Gmail domains are different mailboxes. The cost is bounded by the trial limits (1 location, 5 staff).
- **Erasure path.** `public.forget_trial_claim(secret, user id, email)` removes one person's two records. It is **service_role only** (anon and signed-in users are refused) and is deliberately kept in production: it is the erasure procedure, and the end-to-end cleanup also uses it so repeated test runs keep earning trials. The e2e scripts need `TRIAL_CLAIM_SECRET` in `.env.local`.

## Safety nets
- **Double subscriptions**: before opening Checkout the app asks Stripe whether the customer already has a live subscription and refuses if so; Checkout pages expire after an hour and the same plan chosen twice within 10 minutes returns the same page. If a second paid subscription still arrives (two tabs paid), the webhook cancels the extra one **and refunds its paid invoices automatically** (idempotent: a retry never refunds twice), logs `ALERT: Duplicate subscription ...`, and, if `BILLING_ALERT_EMAIL` is set (needs Resend), emails that address. If a refund could not be made, the alert says "REFUND NEEDED": refund that subscription's charge in the Stripe dashboard by hand.
- **Webhooks are never trusted blindly**: the signature is checked, events with `account` set or from the wrong mode (test/live) are ignored, and the subscription is always re-read from Stripe before it is applied.
- **Reconciliation**: the hourly job (`/api/cron/reminders`) re-reads up to 25 subscriptions from Stripe each run, least recently checked first (`last_reconciled_at`, stamped on every attempt), so a lost webhook is repaired within hours and rows Stripe doesn't know can't block the queue. A subscription Stripe says doesn't exist is only marked (`stripe_missing_at`) and logged, never cancelled automatically (it is also what a wrong or switched API key looks like): query `select org_id, stripe_subscription_id from subscriptions where stripe_missing_at is not null` after switching keys. A sweep never overwrites a newer write (compare-and-set on `updated_at`). It also deletes webhook records older than 90 days.
- **Unpaid subscription past the grace period**: the business is read-only, and Subscribe / Change plan send the owner to **Manage billing** (the Stripe Customer Portal) with an explanation, because the unpaid invoice must be settled there.
- **Grace expiry is sticky (D-018)**: a plan that expired after the failed-payment grace stays `expired` even while Stripe still reports `past_due` (it keeps retrying the card); the hourly job and late webhooks do not flip it back and send no "subscription is active" email. It returns to `active` only when Stripe reports `active` (for example on `invoice.paid`, or after paying in the Customer Portal), or when a different, new subscription replaces it.
- **Duplicate race caveat (S-018 / S-020)**: if two Checkouts complete at the same instant, the loser of the race is re-checked and, when the winner is alive at Stripe, cancelled and refunded. If the winner is already dead at Stripe, the loser is kept (it is the customer's only live plan) and the app tries once more to adopt it; if that also loses a race, the row may look inactive until the owner opens Subscribe or a later webhook or the hourly reconcile arrives.
- **Pending invitations hold a seat**: the Billing meter, the downgrade check and the database all count them the same way.
- **Payment failure emails** go to the owner once, when the problem starts (not on every Stripe retry).
- Only owners see billing details (dates, payment problems). Admins and members only see that the plan isn't active and who to ask.

- **Optional alert address:** `BILLING_ALERT_EMAIL` (see `.env.example`).
- Emails (transactional): trial ending (3 days), trial ended, payment failed, subscription started/changed.
